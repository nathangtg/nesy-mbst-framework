Topic 26: Model-Based Statistical Testing
Problem Statement
Traditional testing techniques often fail to achieve adequate fault detection in
complex software systems. Model-Based Statistical Testing (MBST) offers promise but
lacks systematic evaluation of its effectiveness and applicability.
Proposed Paper Title
"Improving Fault Detection in Software Systems Using Model-Based Statistical Testing
Techniques"
Summary of the Paper
This review paper systematically evaluates Model-Based Statistical Testing
techniques, their fault detection effectiveness, and applicability across different
system types. It compares MBST with traditional testing and identifies best practices
for adoption.
Instructions to Students
Abstract (2 marks)
Summarize MBST concept, effectiveness evidence reviewed, comparison with
traditional testing, and adoption recommendations.
Introduction (4 marks)
• Define Model-Based Statistical Testing (MBST)
• Discuss fault detection challenges
• State research questions
• Present paper structure
Literature Review (4 marks)
Review 15-20 papers on:
• MBST foundations (models, statistical sampling)
• MBST algorithms and tools
• Empirical studies of MBST effectiveness
• Comparison with traditional testing
• MBST in safety-critical systems
Critical Analysis & Findings (4 marks)
• Compare MBST techniques (coverage, efficiency)
• Analyze fault detection improvement metrics
• Identify gaps: scalability, model complexity
• Provide evidence-based best practices
Conclusion & Future Work (4 marks)
• Propose MBST adoption guidelines
• Suggest AI for model generation
• Recommend industrial case studies
